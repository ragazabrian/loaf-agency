Loaf is the creative partner that has your back, not just your brief. We bring commercial thinking, honest challenge and genuine craft – seeing the bigger picture and staying invested in the outcome.

20 years of helping brands bloom

We believe creativity makes businesses stronger. From brand strategy and identity to campaigns and communications, we create work that helps brands matter, campaigns perform and businesses grow.

Loaf is an independent creative agency, specialising in branding, campaigns and digital experiences. We’ve been helping brands realise their potential since 2005.

We work with organisations to build authentic, meaningful brands – and to deliver engaging experiences that connect with the people who matter, inside and out.

We create work that delivers in the moment and builds towards lasting change.

Results

  • Merseyrail Leisure Campaign: 11% YOY increase in ticket sales with a 79% increase in visitors to the events webpage.
  • Grand National Campaign: Raised YOY Opening Day ticket sales by 4%, Ladies Day by 6%, with a complete sellout on Grand National Day.
  • Hatch Manchester Launch: Helped drive footfall of 60,000 unique visitors to Hatch in the three months after launch.
  • Liverpool ONE Xmas Campaign: 12.9% revenue uplift from the previous year and significantly outperformed the national average performance over the festive period by 11.2%.
  • Merseyrail Leisure Campaign 2023: 15% revenue uplift in leisure travel from 2022, 478% increase in social media engagement, 552% more journeys planned online.
  • BAE B2B Campaign: Helped BAE connect and establish relationships with chief contacts at many of the UK’s biggest transport companies.
