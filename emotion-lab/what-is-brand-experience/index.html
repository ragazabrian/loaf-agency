Long live experience

Experience is dead

MD/Co-founder – Alan Houghton · 10 March 2022

The landscape we operate in and what clients need from a creative partner is radically changing, and in turn, our approach has had to evolve. Psychology, data, technology, and innovation are now just as big a part of what we do as the craft and production values that we made our name with. There is a near-constant tension in the advertising industry about our role in people’s lives. We often see ourselves solving problems, finding new solutions, and ultimately, selling — but to what end? This thought is behind our shift towards brand experience. And it’s more than just a buzzword. For us, brand experience is about meeting people where they are. Seeing how the brand interacts with the rest of their life. And ultimately making your brand present in innovative, appropriate, and impactful ways. 

Context matters. Customer journeys are changing and we need to make sure that every touchpoint is one that moves and motivates the customer to act. The brands that succeed are ones who put in the work — because a strong brand foundation is the most important step in working towards a consistent, engaging, and effective brand experience. If you look to the direct-to-consumer successes from the past 10 years, many have been enabled by the unfettered access to their audiences. Need advice on product development? A testbed of 100k is only an Instagram post away. For brands with a clear vision, it’s an easy win. 

That quick win isn’t often replicated by brands who were grandfathered into this digital age. The winners win because it’s natural. The same goes for purpose. Those who succeed with pro-social messaging do so because it’s organic. Every touchpoint is an experience. Every interaction and relationship counts. We believe that brands that commit to engaging people with more empathy and emotion at every opportunity create better brand experiences and more desirable brands. Thankfully, it doesn’t have to be a revolution. It all starts with intent. And this is a space where iterative improvements result in measurable results. 

But the more things change, the more they stay the same. Which is why at the heart of what we do, still remains the drive to deliver work that makes people feel something. Work that motivates people to do something. Work that is beautiful.
