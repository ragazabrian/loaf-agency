What the future holds

Brand experience & social distancing

Creative Director / Co-founder – Dave Mullen · 03 April 2020

We, like many other businesses and our clients, have been facing the challenge of keeping sales continuous whilst delivering an uninterrupted experience and service during the outbreak of Covid-19. It’s clear that we are all yet to discover our new norm, as the world learns how to adapt to our adjusted lifestyles.

Our recent tilt in focus as an agency to brand experience, both improving and unifying it, has exposed us to more relevant and more important questions about what our clients actually need, what we as a creative agency are actually here to do, and what the value of this work is.

Over the last few weeks we have been working with our clients to understand how their brand experiences can be maintained, whilst their ability to connect and engage with their audience is being put to the test. With the focus of attention quite rightly on the health and wellbeing of family and loved ones - the concerns of our clients are real - how do they best align with their customers and spend budgets effectively now and over the coming months.

Our aim is to share our experience and creative expertise to provide solutions that are both swift and insightful. We believe there are a range of new opportunities available for brands to strengthen their visibility and conversion - whilst also helping to build their brand for the future.

Our experience in helping brands better engage with customers has allowed us to hone in on the challenges our clients are currently facing, and support them in a range of different ways. We have learned a lot in the last few weeks, and we wanted to share some of our thoughts and opinions on what brands should be considering in relation to their brand experience…

Don’t expect ‘normal’
As the lockdown eases, some of the more successful changes in how we work and live will be here to stay. Many consumers won’t want the old ‘normal’, so it’s essential brands really understand what people actually want. Many may find that this is very different to what their insight and research of the past suggests.

Change gear with content
With the increased amount of time consumers are spending online, brands need to seize the opportunity to be more present. There should be a focus on producing a range of relevant content that is based on storytelling and adding value to lives, not just gaining likes and sales. This more than ever, is a time to stand out from the crowd.

Create more engaging online destinations
Digital media has quickly become a leader in enabling social interactions and sales. Social distancing and limitations in place restrict the ability to meet your customers face to face and engage in person. From shopping on the high street to viewing properties, the needs and expectations of online experiences have risen - and every online touchpoint needs be unique, memorable and useful.

Be open with your agency
Relationships are essential to success. Now is the time to sit down with your creative agency and openly discuss the challenges your company is facing right now. A good creative agency will listen, be honest about the issues it can help with, and work with you to solve them.

Have a plan
This sounds obvious, but this is a very unique situation that requires a unique plan. Even if your activity is already predominantly digital, a shift in focus and how you interact is important to be relevant in the current market. What was important to consumers a month ago, may not be so important now.

Stay safe everyone.
