A Google A.I and Aphex twin mash up

Speech synthesis

Head of Technology - Andy Buchan · 11 May 2022

Whilst exploring Google's A.I text-to-speech service we came across what is known as Speech Synthesis Markup Language (SSML). This is the part of voice design which allows designers to make it feel as human-like as possible. Using SSML, we started adding small nuonses to improve the realism to what is essentially a machine made voice. Following this, we then hooked it up to Google's API which means you can personalise messaging.

Continuing on the A.I path, we then came across the incredible work by Alex Bainter, who has created the Ambient Generative Music piece called https://generative.fm/. Using A.I Alex created a tool which produces endless Aphex Twin inspired songs which are endless, and completely unique for every play of each track.

The opportunities for using A.I in the creative world is huge. By understanding the power that this kind technology can have opens up all kinds of new ideation avenues for brands.
