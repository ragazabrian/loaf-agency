A digital home for Manchester’s new skyline

  • Client: Renaker
  • Project: New Jackson Website
  • Type: Brand
  • Services: Content Structuring, Interactive Design, Research, UI + UX Design, Web Build

Bringing a world-class neighbourhood to life online

We designed and built the official website for New Jackson – a landmark skyscraper district, redefining city living in Manchester. The platform captures the district’s vision, energy and ambition, providing a seamless digital experience that showcases its architecture, amenities and vibrant community.

Bringing a world-class neighbourhood to life online

Developed by Renaker, New Jackson represents the next evolution of urban living – a thriving new district of residential towers, green spaces and community-driven amenities. Our challenge was to translate this large-scale vision into an elegant, intuitive website that brings the place to life for residents, investors and partners alike.

From UX to UI and build, we created a responsive modern platform that balances design precision with ease of navigation. Clean transitions, interactive mapping and immersive imagery combine to tell the story of a district in motion – one that is shaping Manchester’s skyline and redefining city living.

Structured around clear narrative sections – the site captures the spirit of place from its architecture and sustainability to the lifestyle it enables. The result is a world-class digital showcase for a world-class neighbourhood – a destination built for connection, discovery and aspiration.
