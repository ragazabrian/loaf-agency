While our understanding around the importance of good sleep has progressed, the industry has been left behind. When we were asked to overhaul the architecture of Trendsetter stable of brands we uncovered a new territory and commercial opportunity for them. This Is Sleep is an industry first, tailor made way to buy sleep. Personal, holistic and contemporary for today's consumer.

Reimagining sleep through insight and innovation

Trendsetter International are a leading bedding brand, with partners including John Lewis and Harrods. They initially tasked us with a strategic brand development project – identifying any new opportunities and reviewing the role and architecture of their current brands.

We started by collaborating with the senior team to define our collective vision and ambition. Through this process we uncovered both a human and commercial insight that lead us to create a new brand that would open up a new opportunity, and allow for a re-structure that would make sense.

Research showed that competition was cluttered but shallow. A flourishing start-up mattress industry was losing steam thanks to too many upstarts and not enough differentiation or personalisation. We uncovered a space for someone with real roots and a diverse heritage in sleep and bedding. One that understands what it takes to get great sleep, as well as it’s importance.

So we created This is Sleep – a new brand offering and total sleep solution, built on product development, sleep theory and the conviction that great sleep is a practice, not a product. Everything was built from scratch, including a complete sleep diagnostic e-commerce system. This focus on the benefits, as well as the holistic and personal, allowed shoppers to understand, nurture and value their sleep.
