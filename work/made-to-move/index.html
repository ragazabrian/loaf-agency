Actioning an Olympic Legacy.

Sheffield Olympic Legacy Park was entering a new phase of development driven forward by Scarborough International Group. After a decade of successful partnerships and investment, this next stage of commercial growth required a strong and distinctive brand rooted in an active legacy.

Actioning an Olympic Legacy.

The London 2012 Olympic and Paralympic Games set a global stage for the UK and Sheffield. A legacy of sport, health, access, and opportunity. Sheffield Olympic Legacy Park is putting that legacy to work as the only Olympic legacy park outside of a host city. The brand needed to accommodate community engagement, research and innovation, education, commercial development and more. Our solution was rooted in what makes the Olympics great: proximity, performance, and potential.

With investment in commercial innovation, research, and education, this is a place ‘made to move’. The brand captures the energy and enthusiasm of elite sport without excluding the person next door. Sheffield Olympic Legacy Park is ready to take its place on the national and global stage as an exemplar place for world-class innovation in sport, health and wellbeing.

“Sheffield Olympic Legacy Park is a complex brief, in terms of its structure, composition and diverse audience groups but we have been extremely impressed by how well the team at Loaf got to grips with the various component parts in a relatively short space of time to really crystalise who the Park is for and what it stands for. Not only that but their creative outputs are fresh, innovative and really reflect the ethos of the brand.”

— Nicola Bulley, Group Head of Marketing & Communications - Scarborough Group International
