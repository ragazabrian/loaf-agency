Rewiring the future of property transactions

A new brand and proposition for NPTN by LMS, a bold new approach to transforming the way property transactions happen. We created a brand that makes complex infrastructure feel simple, human, and full of momentum, built around one powerful idea: collaboration that rewires the system.

Rewiring the future of property transactions

The way we buy and sell homes is broken. For NPTN, the ambition was to change that, connecting lenders, law firms, estate agents, brokers, technology providers, and customers through one smarter flow of data.

Our challenge was to make a complex piece of industry infrastructure feel immediate and compelling. Rather than presenting NPTN as another platform or technology product, we positioned it as a fundamental shift in how the property market works: open, connected, faster, and built for everyone.

The visual identity is brought to life through a dynamic world of connected forms, movement and data. A flexible graphic system gives the brand a sense of constant flow, while bold typography and confident messaging make the proposition clear without losing the human story behind it.

The result is a brand designed not simply to launch a new network, but to help move an entire industry forward. Because NPTN isn’t about digitising the existing system. It’s about rebuilding it for what comes next.
